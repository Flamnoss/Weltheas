## Lore
Ezekiel Kezra was a boy who was unloved by his parents since birth. He was thrown out of the house onto the streets. 
He was found by this lovely lady around 50 years old named Rosa Chesterfield. She raised him as her own. At age 10 he worked as a blacksmith. 
They were poor but they were still able to live off of a low income. At age 16, Ezekiel was arriving home after a long day of work. 
It was night time. He had noticed some police officers weren’t at their post. He then saw all of the police officers in a bar which instantly frightened him. 
Since the job of the police officers was to hold the post on the outskirts of the village. 
Since they weren't there he ran home to see Rosa dying because of an attack from monsters. Rosa died in Ezekiel's hands. 
A lot of houses were attacked by the monsters. From that point on he lived alone and hated the police and monsters.

6 months later he moved to a more populated city for a better life. He was working as a blacksmith again but it was a lot harder of work. 
He could barely afford rent and food. He was walking home one day and saw a lady with a baby being attacked by some police officers. 
He rushed over and fought off the 3 police officers and defeated them. He told the lady to run away. 
Ezekiel looked over his shoulder and saw they called for backup and he started running to the harbor since he knew he wasn’t safe. 
He saw this big ship and ran on without being seen and hid in it. The police officers lost him. 
He decided to stay on the ship so he could leave the city since he was wanted.

The ship he wandered on was those of the Wealthians.
