1. **Membership Requirement:**
To contribute to the Weltheas wiki, you must be an active member of the Weltheas nation. This ensures that all content is relevant and true to the nation’s identity and values.

2. **Respect and Collaboration:**
All contributors must be respectful to one another. Disagreements should be handled constructively, and personal attacks or harassment will not be tolerated. 

3. **Accuracy and Consistency:**
Contributions should be accurate and consistent with the lore and rules of Weltheas. When in doubt, verify facts with other sources or discuss with fellow members to ensure the information is aligned with the countries lore.

4. **No Spam or Vandalism:**
Please refrain from adding irrelevant or disruptive content. This includes spam, inappropriate images, or deliberate changes meant to mislead or confuse other users. All edits should enhance the wiki’s value.

5. **Edit Summary:**
When making significant edits, please provide a brief summary of the changes made. This helps other contributors understand the rationale behind the edits and ensures transparency in the editing process.

6. **Respect Privacy:**
Do not include personal information about other members or any content that could compromise the privacy of individuals. Respect the boundaries set by the community and be mindful of sensitive topics.

7. **No Plagiarism:**
All content should be original or properly cited. Avoid copying content from external sources without permission, and always give credit to creators where due.

8. **Follow the Arcamere Rules and GitHub Terms of Service:**
The Weltheas wiki is hosted on GitHub Pages. All contributors must follow both the Arcamere community rules and GitHub’s Terms of Service. Any violations of these rules, including misuse of the platform, will result in removal of access to the wiki.
